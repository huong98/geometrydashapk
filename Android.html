Lag can be a real game-killer, especially in a precision platformer like Geometry Dash. First, close all background apps to free up RAM – this alone often solves the problem. Then, dive into Geometry Dash's settings and enable "Low Detail Mode" in the Options menu. This reduces particle effects and visual complexity, which can significantly boost performance on older devices.

If you're still experiencing stuttering, try clearing the app cache (Settings > Apps > Geometry Dash > Clear Cache) and make sure you have at least 1GB of free storage. Some users also find that disabling "Smooth Fix" in the game settings helps on certain devices. If your Android version is older than 5.0, consider upgrading your OS, as newer versions handle graphics more efficiently.
