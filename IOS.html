Step 1: Install AltServer on Your Computer

  1. Download AltServer from the official AltStore website (altstore.io) for your operating system.
  2. Install it. On Windows, you will also need to install iCloud from Apple's website (not the Microsoft Store version).
  3. Run AltServer. It will appear as an icon in your system tray (Windows) or menu bar (macOS).
  4. Connect your iPhone/iPad to your computer with a USB cable. Trust the computer on your device if prompted.
  5. Open iTunes (Windows) or Finder (macOS), select your device, and enable "Sync with this [iDevice] over Wi-Fi."

Step 2: Install AltStore on Your iOS Device

  1. With your device connected, click the AltServer icon on your computer, select "Install AltStore," and choose your device.
  2. You will be prompted to enter your Apple ID and password. This is sent directly to Apple to sign the app and is not stored by AltStore.
  3. On your iOS device, go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management.
  4. You will see your Apple ID under "Developer App." Tap it and then tap "Trust."
  5. AltStore is now installed on your device.

Step 3: Sideload the Geometry Dash IPA

  1. You will need to find a trusted source for the Geometry Dash .ipa file. Download it to your iPhone/iPad (e.g., in your Files app).
  2. Open the downloaded .ipa file. Tap the "Share" button and select "AltStore" from the list of apps.
  3. AltStore will open and begin installing the app. You may need to sign in with your Apple ID again.
  4. Once installed, Geometry Dash will appear on your home screen.
